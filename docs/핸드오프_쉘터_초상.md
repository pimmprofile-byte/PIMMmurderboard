### 3-1. 차서령 · 31 · 여

前 최연소 국회의원, 재벌가 딸. 집안이 이 약을 만들어 뿌렸고, 그녀는 반대했다가
혼자 버려졌습니다. 말이 험하고 직설적입니다.

```
A woman of thirty-one, standing in the orange lamplight of a campground
management office at night. She wears a worn wool coat over layered clothes,
everything faded khaki and earth brown, the fabric visibly old and mended.

Her posture is straight-backed and formal in a way the rest of the room is not
— she carries herself like someone who used to be introduced before she walked
into a room. She looks directly at the viewer. There is cold amusement left on
her face, the tail end of a sneer that never fully leaves.

One hand is raised to hold the collar of her coat closed. That hand is the
detail that matters: it is well kept, the nails clean and even, the skin
unworked — a hand from a life that never lifted anything. It does not match
the coat.

Behind her the office is dark and out of focus: a desk, a lamp burning warm
orange, a window with a faint sickly green glow far outside in the night sky.
The green stays outside and never touches her face; all the light on her is
the warm orange of the lamp.
```

### 3-2. 한도겸 · 26 · 남 ★

연구원. 항노화를 연구했고 그 논문이 이 재앙의 기반이 됐습니다. 할머니를 지키려고
쓴 것이었고, 할머니는 그 약으로 죽었습니다.

```
A young man who reads as about eighteen, though he is twenty-six. His face is
smooth and unlined and boyish, the face of a high school student — he has been
testing a suppressant on himself, and it has pushed him the opposite way from
everyone else.

His eyes do not match that face. They are exhausted, older than the rest of
him, set deep. The mismatch between the age of his face and the age of his
eyes is the point of this portrait, and it should be uncomfortable to look at.
He is not cute and not charming. He is tired.

He is thin and slight, in a worn hoodie with a battered backpack strap over
one shoulder. Both hands grip that strap in front of his chest; the fingernails
are bitten short. Wire-framed glasses. He looks directly at the viewer.

Behind him the generator shed is dark and out of focus: machinery, cable runs,
a single warm orange work light. Faint sickly green sits far away in the night
outside, never reaching his face.
```

### 3-3. 노강일 · 42 · 남

前 특전사 상사. 모두가 떠날 때 명령을 어기고 남았고, 남아서 지킨 사람들에게
배신당했습니다. 사람은 이제 됐다고 생각합니다.

```
A large, powerfully built man of forty-two, standing at the tent ground at
night. He is not in uniform — a worn field jacket over a plain shirt, olive
drab and dust — but he is unmistakably built and trained like a soldier, the
shoulders square and the back straight without effort.

His face is blank. No anger, no sorrow, nothing offered. And unlike everyone
else in this set, he is not looking at the viewer: his eyes are turned slightly
away, off to the side, watching something outside the frame.

His arms are crossed over his chest. The backs of his hands are thick with old
callus, the knuckles worked and scarred — twenty years of it.

Behind him the tent ground is dark and out of focus: a few standing tents, a
clothesline strung between poles with washing still on it, one warm orange lamp
low to the ground. Faint sickly green in the far night sky, never touching him.
```

### 3-4. 인혜숙 · 52 · 여 ★ — **진범**

前 국정원 블랙요원. 이십 년을 이름 없이 살았고, 퇴직 후 거리에서 몇 해를 보냈습니다.
사람을 제압하는 법을 아는 사람이 그렇게 살았습니다.

> **여기가 이번 작업에서 제일 어려운 한 장입니다.** 세 가지가 **동시에** 성립해야 합니다.
> 하나라도 무너지면 트릭이 죽습니다.
>
> 1. **무해해 보일 것** — 플레이어가 「이 사람은 아니겠지」 하고 넘겨야 합니다
> 2. **조로** — 쉰둘인데 예순으로
> 3. **왼쪽 손목이 보이지 않을 것** — 소매가 손등까지 내려와 있어야 합니다

```
A small, stooped woman who reads as about sixty, though she is fifty-two. She
stands in the orange lamplight of a camp kitchen at night, in a shapeless
padded jacket, faded and much washed.

She must look completely harmless. Her shoulders are rounded, her frame is
small, her expression is mild and patient — the face of someone who listens and
does not speak. Nothing about her posture is threatening. She looks at the
viewer gently.

Her hair is dyed flat black and combed neatly, and at the parting the roots have
come in white. She does this herself and it matters to her.

Her hands are folded together low in front of her. **The sleeves of her jacket
are pulled all the way down over her wrists and halfway across the backs of her
hands, so no part of either wrist is visible.** The hands themselves are the
one thing that does not fit the rest of her: steady, precise, strong, the grip
sure — not the hands of a frail old woman. That contradiction should be quiet
enough to miss on a first look and obvious on a second.

Behind her the camp kitchen is dark and out of focus: shelving, stacked pots, a
washing basin, one warm orange lamp. Faint sickly green far away in the night
outside, never touching her face.
```

## 4. 리젝 체크리스트

제미나이는 금지어를 못 받으니 **사람이 걸러야 합니다.**

**네 장 공통**

- [ ] **포토리얼이다** — 잉크 선이 없으면 그 시점에서 리젝입니다
- [ ] 애니풍·수채·유화풍이다 (그래픽노블 잉크여야 합니다)
- [ ] 얼굴이 화면 중앙이나 아래에 있다 (**상단 1/3**이어야 합니다)
- [ ] 손이 안 보인다
- [ ] **녹색이 얼굴에 닿았다** — 녹색은 창밖·먼 하늘에만
- [ ] 배경이 선명하다 (흐리고 어두워야 인물이 삽니다)
- [ ] 하단이 안 어둡다 (이름표가 얹힐 자리입니다)
- [ ] 글자·로고가 보인다
- [ ] **무기를 들고 있다** — 넷 다 아무것도 들지 않습니다
- [ ] 너무 깨끗하거나 너무 지저분하다 (몇 달 살림한 사람들입니다)

**인물별**

- [ ] 차서령 — 손이 거칠다. **손만은 관리돼 있어야** 합니다
- [ ] 한도겸 — **귀엽다.** 얼굴은 어리되 눈은 지쳐 있어야 합니다. 실제로 십대로만 보여도 리젝
- [ ] 노강일 — **정면을 본다.** 넷 중 혼자 시선이 비껴야 합니다
- [ ] 인혜숙 — **손목이 조금이라도 보인다 → 무조건 리젝**
- [ ] 인혜숙 — 강해 보인다, 눈빛이 날카롭다, 자세가 곧다 (전부 리젝)
- [ ] 인혜숙 — 배경에 **염색약 통이 보인다** (그건 조사카드에서 찾는 물건입니다)
