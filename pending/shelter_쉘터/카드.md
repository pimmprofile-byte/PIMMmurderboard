## 1. 사건 현장 · 흔적 5장

### A1 · 국성수의 시신 → `shelter_card_A1.webp`
**관리동 · 책상 앞 바닥**

> An elderly man lying face down on the floor in front of a desk in a small
> concrete office, seen from above at a low angle. **His face is turned to one
> side and it is peaceful — gently smiling.** Symmetrical pressure marks at his
> throat, left and right, and nothing else: no weapon, no blood. **One sleeve of
> his outer jacket is torn open in a single long vertical rip, and the cuff
> button is missing from that sleeve.** The knuckles of his right hand are
> swollen and red — he gripped something very hard. **Dried mud on his shoes and
> trouser cuffs, and none of that mud anywhere on the floor around him.** On the
> desk above, a framed photograph lying face down. Warm lamplight. Restrained,
> not gory.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 온화하게 웃는 얼굴 · 세로로 찢어진 한쪽 소매와 없어진 단추 · 벌겋게 부은 오른손 관절 · **신발엔 흙이 있는데 바닥엔 없는 것**
**리젝:** 고통스러운 표정이면 리젝. 이 얼굴이 카드의 전부입니다

### B7 · 식탁 밑에 떨어진 것 → `shelter_card_B7.webp`
**취사장 · 식탁 밑 바닥**

> The floor under a long wooden table in an open-sided cooking shelter, close and
> from above. **A small scatter of white powder on the boards beside a table
> leg, and one half-crushed tablet lying in it, its markings worn off.** There
> are sweep marks through the powder — somebody brushed it away in a hurry and
> the broom never reached behind the leg. Lamplight from the table above throws
> the leg's shadow across the spill. Nothing else on this floor is dirty.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 급하게 쓸다 만 자국 · 식탁 다리 뒤에 남은 가루와 알약 조각

### C5 · 던져진 신분증 → `shelter_card_C5.webp`
**발전동 · 기름통과 벽 사이**

> The narrow gap between an oil drum and a concrete wall behind a generator.
> **A laminated ID card lies down in the gap, and there is a long skid mark
> through the dust where it slid in** — thrown, not dropped. The photo on it
> shows an older man, and it is scuffed; the printed fields are illegible
> strokes. **The laminate surface is clouded with a smeared handprint**, the kind
> left by a wet hand holding something a long time. Cold shadow in the gap, one
> warm lamp above.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 먼지 위에 길게 남은 «미끄러진» 자국 — 흘린 게 아니라 던진 자리 · 코팅 겉면에 뭉개진 손자국
**리젝:** 카드가 반듯하게 놓여 있으면 리젝

### D5 · 공터에 남은 자국 → `shelter_card_D5.webp`
**텐트터 · 텐트 사이 공터**

> A patch of dry dirt in the open ground between canvas tents, seen from above.
> **One area is scuffed and churned wide and flat — the width two people make
> when they are locked together for a while.** No footprints at all: the dirt is
> too dry to take them. **A folding chair lies knocked over at the edge, its
> canvas back split in a single vertical tear.** And in the churned dirt, **one
> small cuff button from a man's jacket**, half pressed into the ground. Tent
> walls close on all sides. Night, one lamp.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 발자국이 아니라 «넓게 뭉개진» 자국 · 세로로 찢어진 접이의자 등받이 · 흙 속의 소매 단추 (A1의 없어진 단추와 짝입니다)

### F2 · 능선의 무덤을 열다 → `shelter_card_F2.webp`
**숲길 · 길목 무덤**

> A small cairn grave on a forest trail, opened. The stones lie pulled aside, a
> shovel standing in loose soil. **The hole is empty right down to the bottom —
> no urn, no cloth, no ash, nothing at all.** The soil is soft and has never been
> tamped. A plain wooden marker leans at the head: **a date carved into it, and
> the name space left blank except for one single stroke, started and abandoned.**
> **Behind the grave, a tin box has been lifted out and set open on the ground,
> a stack of letters inside, the paper worn.** The ground in front of the grave
> is trodden glossy from daily visits. Beyond, the ridge opens north and a thin
> column of sickly green smoke rises from a distant mountain — faint, far away,
> touching nothing.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 바닥까지 텅 빈 무덤 · 첫 획에서 멈춘 이름 자리 · 무덤 뒤에서 나온 양철 편지 상자 · 원경의 녹색 연기
**리젝:** 무덤에 뭐라도 들어 있으면 리젝. 「무덤이 아니라 무덤처럼 보이게 해둔 자리」가 전부입니다

## 2. 서류·상자·소지품 6장

### A8 · 부치지 않은 봉투 둘 → `shelter_card_A8.webp`
**관리동 · 부치지 않은 봉투 둘**

> Two envelopes laid side by side on a desk under a lamp, **both still sealed
> shut and both without a single postmark, stamp or handling mark** — neither has
> ever been sent. One is thin and official; a printed form has been slid halfway
> out of it, with **one field on the form blacked out with heavy ink** while a
> printed serial block above it survives. The other envelope is fatter; a
> certificate and a second sheet are half out of it, one of them carrying **a
> single round rubber stamp impression** and a line of doctor's handwriting in the
> margin. All writing illegible. Warm lamplight, hard shadows under the paper
> edges.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 한 번도 안 부친 봉투 둘 · 먹으로 지운 칸 하나 · 도장 자국 하나
**리젝:** 글자가 읽히면 리젝

### E2 · 창고 안쪽의 상자 둘 → `shelter_card_E2.webp`
**창고 · 선반 안쪽 상자 둘**

> Two boxes taken down from the back of a storeroom shelf and opened on the
> floor. **The first holds women's clothes, shoes and a hairpin, kept clean under
> a sheet of plastic and recently disturbed** — the plastic folded back, a
> sleeve lifted. **A cloth name tag is stitched inside the collar of the top
> garment** (the writing illegible). **The second is a velvet case, pressed flat
> under a blanket, and it is open: a black star-shaped badge sits in one of its
> two moulded recesses, and the other recess is empty** — the lining still holds
> the shape of what used to be there. One warm lamp on the floor beside them.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 최근에 누가 열어본 자국 · 옷깃의 이름표 · **자리는 둘인데 배지는 하나** — 이게 이 카드의 심장입니다
**리젝:** 배지가 둘이면 리젝

### E3 · 출고 전표와 사업 목록 → `shelter_card_E3.webp`
**창고 · 서류 상자**

> A bundle of old delivery slips fanned out on the floor beside a document box.
> Every slip is ruled the same and the item column is nothing but **symbols, not
> words**; the destination field is **the same set of coordinates on every single
> slip**, week after week. The sender's name is **blacked out on all of them**
> while a printed logistics code survives beneath. **Clipped to the front of the
> bundle is a single sheet listing twenty ruled lines of programme names, and one
> line alone has these slips tied to it with a bracket in pen.** Under the box, a
> second file, most of its page blacked out, only a table of contents surviving —
> **and one name in it blacked out halfway, one character left showing.** All
> text illegible. One lamp, hard side light.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 전부 같은 수령지 · 지워진 발송사와 남은 코드 · 스무 줄 중 한 줄에만 걸린 표시 · 지우다 만 이름 한 글자
**리젝:** 글자가 읽히면 리젝. 「지워진 자리」와 「남은 자리」의 대비만 보이면 됩니다

### G2 · 인소연의 가방 → `shelter_card_G2_insoyeon.webp`
**소지품 · 인소연 · 가방**

> A shoulder bag emptied onto a table. **Four or five folded cloth banners, the
> fold lines worn deep from being opened and closed many times.** On top of them,
> **one banner still rolled up around its pole.** The pole is thicker than a
> banner pole should be — **and where the wooden sleeve has slipped, segments of

> Warm lamplight from one side, the table dark beyond.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 나무 소매 안의 강철 마디 — 접이식이라는 것 · 한 자리만 반들거리게 닳은 손잡이 · 이음매의 눌린 자국
**리젝:** 현수막 글자가 읽히면 리젝

### G2 · 정수호의 가방 → `shelter_card_G2_jeongsuho.webp`
**소지품 · 정수호 · 가방**

> An old backpack emptied onto a table, and its contents **sorted into two
> deliberate groups with a palm-width of bare table between them so they cannot
> mix.** On the left: **six identical brown glass vials**, some with the seal
> broken, one nearly empty, and beside them a syringe and cotton swabs. On the
> right: **exactly two vials of a different shape with thicker stoppers, both

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 두 무리 사이의 «손바닥만 한 빈자리» · 왼쪽 여섯 / 오른쪽 둘 · 모양과 마개가 다른 것

### G2 · 이민희의 가방 → `shelter_card_G2_riminhui.webp`
**소지품 · 이민희 · 가방**

> A small handbag tipped out on a table, **and only one thing has come out of
> it.** A single photocopied bankbook page, **grey and washed out because it is a
> copy of a copy, with four deep fold creases crossing it.** The transaction
> column is ruled and almost entirely empty: **one deposit line at the top, and
> below it nothing but blank ruled rows running all the way down.** The balance
> figure at the right has a great many digits. Everything illegible except the
> shape of the ruling. **The table around the bag is bare** — the emptiness beside
> it is part of the picture. Warm lamplight.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 나온 게 종이 한 장뿐이라는 것 · 입금 한 줄 아래로 전부 빈 칸 · 자릿수가 긴 잔액 · **가방 주변의 빈 탁자**
**리젝:** 가방에서 다른 물건이 여럿 나오면 리젝
