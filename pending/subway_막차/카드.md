## 1. 프롬프트

### A1 · 선로의 시신 → `subway_card_A1.webp`
**선로 · 승강장 끝**

> A man lying face down across the sleepers of a subway track, seen from the
> platform edge above. He is in a cheap suit. **His outstretched hand has come to
> rest touching the third rail, and the fingertips are burnt black** — the one
> hard fact in the picture. No blood, no distortion; handled with restraint. Only
> orange sodium light down here, the fluorescent glare of the platform cutting
> off in a hard line above him. Ballast, dark cable trays, the tunnel mouth
> beyond.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 제3궤조에 닿은 손끝의 검은 그을림 — 감전사라는 것
**리젝:** 피가 보이면 리젝 · 얼굴이 정면으로 보이면 리젝

### A2 · 타살의 흔적 → `subway_card_A2.webp`
**선로 · 승강장 끝**

> The back of a man's jacket, close and large, seen from above. **Pressed into
> the fabric between the shoulder blades, the clear shape of two hands, splayed,
> the dust of the jacket disturbed exactly where palms and fingers pushed** —
> shoved, hard, from behind. Below, at the edge of frame, one of his hands is
> turned up: **the nails are broken and dirty, and caught between two fingers is
> a single fine thread, loosely spun, plainly pulled out of knitted wool.**
> Orange sodium, hard raking light so the handprint reads.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 등판의 «손자국 두 개» · 손가락 사이의 가는 털실 올 (E2 목도리와 짝입니다)
**리젝:** 손자국이 뭉개져서 안 읽히면 리젝

### A3 · 서동철의 소지품 → `subway_card_A3.webp`
**선로 · 승강장 끝**

> Three things laid out on a sheet of paper on the platform floor: a worn leather
> wallet lying open, **a thick hand-written debt ledger, its pages swollen and
> curled from use, open to a spread ruled into a long column of names and
> figures** (all illegible), and **a smartphone, face up, its screen lit with a
> lock pattern grid and a stack of unread message banners piled at the top.**
> The message banners are drawn as blank grey bars — no readable text. Flat
> fluorescent light from directly above, cold and unkind.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 이름이 빼곡한 장부 · 알림이 쌓인 잠긴 휴대폰
**리젝:** 장부나 알림의 글자가 읽히면 리젝

### A5 · 선로로 내려간 흔적 → `subway_card_A5.webp`
**선로 · 승강장 끝**

> The bottom of a steel ladder at the end of a subway platform, where it meets
> the ballast, seen close. **Fresh scuffs on the rungs, and on the ballast below,
> shoe prints pressed into the track grease.** The greasy prints come off the
> stones, **climb back up the ladder, and continue onto the platform concrete as
> two or three fading black marks that stop.** Beside them **a long smeared drag
> mark, soft-edged, as if cloth was pulled across the grease.** Whoever it was
> stepped down, stayed a moment, and came back up. Sodium below, fluorescent
> above, the boundary running right through the picture.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 내려갔다 «다시 올라온» 방향 · 천이 끌린 자국
**리젝:** 발자국이 선로 쪽으로만 나 있으면 리젝

### B1 · 내려간 셔터 → `subway_card_B1.webp`
**승강장 (개찰구 밖)**

> A corrugated steel rolling shutter closed all the way to the floor across a
> station exit, seen head on and close, filling most of the frame. **The bottom
> rail has landed hard and sits flush on the concrete; a folded paper handbill
> is pinched under it and torn.** Beside the shutter, **a control panel with its
> cover open and a key switch in it**, and the shutter's guide rail scraped
> bright at one point where it came down out of true. Flat fluorescent light,
> the corrugations throwing a hard rhythm of shadow. Nobody in frame.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 완전히 바닥까지 내려온 셔터 · 밑에 끼어 찢어진 종이(내려온 시각이 갑작스러웠다는 것)
**리젝:** 틈이 있으면 리젝

### C1 · 불 켜진 편의점 → `subway_card_C1.webp`
**역내 편의점**

> A convenience store counter at night, seen from a customer's side. A card
> terminal, a receipt printer with **a long curl of till roll hanging out of
> it**, a cup of instant noodles gone cold, a small camera on the wall behind
> aimed straight down at this counter. Behind the counter, a wall of lit
> refrigerator cases — packaging drawn as colour blocks only, no lettering.
> **The light here is white and strong and it stops dead a few metres out**,
> beyond which the station is flat grey. Nobody in frame.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 계산대를 겨눈 CCTV · 길게 뽑혀 나온 영수증 롤 · 여기만 밝다는 대비

### D3 · 한지운의 차용증 → `subway_card_D3.webp`
**관제실 · 사물함**

> The inside of a steel staff locker, its door swung open, a uniform jacket
> pushed aside. From behind the jacket, **a promissory note has been pulled out
> and unfolded — official form paper, ruled fields, a red thumb-stamp pressed at
> the foot** (the only colour in frame), all writing illegible. Under it, **a
> phone lying face up with a column of demand messages on screen, drawn as blank
> grey bars, one after another after another** — the sheer number is the point.
> Cold monitor glow from off frame, deep shadow inside the locker.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 붉은 지장 하나 · 셀 수 없이 쌓인 독촉 메시지의 «양»
**리젝:** 메시지 글자가 읽히면 리젝

### E2 · 윤미래의 목도리와 장갑 → `subway_card_E2.webp`
**화장실 · 연결통로 · 휴지통**

> A knitted wool scarf and a pair of gloves pulled out of a station waste bin and
> laid on the tiled floor. **One end of the scarf has unravelled badly — a run of
> loose yarn hangs off it and the weave has opened into a gap.** Track ballast
> grit is caught in the wool, **and both the scarf and the glove palms are marked
> with black track grease.** Beside them the bin stands with its lid tipped back.
> Hard flat fluorescent light on white tile, no shadows to hide in.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 심하게 풀린 한쪽 올 (A2에서 시신이 쥔 올과 같은 실이어야 합니다 — **두 장의 실 색을 반드시 맞추세요**) · 선로 자갈과 궤도 기름
**리젝:** 목도리가 멀쩡하면 리젝

### E3 · 취재수첩과 미행 사진 → `subway_card_E3.webp`
**화장실 · 연결통로**

> The contents of a shoulder bag spread on a tiled floor. **A stack of
> photographs, dozens of them, all of the same middle-aged man taken without his
> knowledge** — through a car window, from across a street, from a doorway, one
> of him asleep. Faces are drawn small and turned away; the point is the **sheer
> repetition**, the same figure over and over from too far away. Beside them a
> spiral notebook open face up, **the margins filled with the same short phrase
> written again and again in the same hand** (illegible strokes, but clearly one
> phrase repeated). A camera with its strap coiled. Hard fluorescent light.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 같은 사람만 수십 장이라는 «반복» · 여백에 반복해서 쓴 한 구절
**리젝:** 사진이 몇 장뿐이면 리젝. 집착이 보여야 합니다

### F2 · 위조된 기자 신분 → `subway_card_F2.webp`
**개찰구 · 매표소**

> Two things side by side on a stainless steel counter. **A press ID card and a
> business card, both visibly cheap — the lamination bubbled at one corner, the
> printing slightly off-register with a colour fringe at the edges of every
> line**, the photo dull. And beside them, taken from the inner fold of a wallet,
> **a small newspaper obituary clipping, old, softened and browned along its
> folds, its column of type illegible but its shape unmistakable.** The clipping
> has been carried for years; the ID was made last week. That contrast is the
> picture. Flat fluorescent light from above.

**꼭 보여야 하는 것:** 인쇄가 어긋난 조악한 신분증 · 오래 품고 다닌 낡은 부고 스크랩 · **둘의 «나이» 차이**
**리젝:** 신분증이 잘 만들어져 보이면 리젝
